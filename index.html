About

I am a founder. I co-founded Nuffle Labs, a spin-out from NEAR Protocol, where we raised $13 million led by Electric Capital, grew to 14 people, and built infrastructure across data availability, shared security and multichain restaking. Nuffle later became MoreMarkets, where we built financial-market products used by more than 4,000 people, with over $45 million in deposits. Before that, I worked in partner engineering at NEAR Protocol.

I started out in research at Davidson College, working on virtual reality and embodiment with Tabitha C. Peck. Our study on the collocation of virtual and physical hands won Best Poster at IEEE VR 2019, and the follow-up work was published in IEEE TVCG. I also spent time in a molecular biology lab, working on riboswitch discovery.

I studied robotics and AI at Imperial College London, running a second master’s at Georgia Tech at the same time, which I later discontinued. I am now working on physical AI, financial markets and real-world risk.
