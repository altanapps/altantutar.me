Which Humanoid

May 2026

I built this because I kept trying to compare humanoid robots and couldn’t. The specifications live scattered across press releases, launch videos and PDFs, each in whatever units the manufacturer preferred that week.

So I catalogued them, 311 at the last count. You can put any two side by side on price, payload, height, walking speed and funding, filter by use case, and watch a ticker of shipments and deployments as they land.

Building it made clear how early this still is. Of the 311, only 68 are shipping. Only 33 publish a price. Only 31 have a customer deployment anyone can verify. The cheapest robot you can actually buy is the Unitree R1 at $4,900; the most expensive I found was $250,000.

If I’ve missed one, submit it. If you find a bug, tell me and I’ll fix it the same day.
